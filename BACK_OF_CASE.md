# Final Evolution Lab — Back of the Game Case

**One line:** Your movement, audited. Your readiness, your edge.

---

## Back-of-case summary (short)

**FINAL EVOLUTION LAB** is a performance-readiness app and game in one. Run a **System Scan** to get your **PRQ** (Performance Readiness Quotient), customize your avatar, and unlock a training program that matches your level. Then hit the **Arena**: dunk contests, penalty shootouts, home run derby, kick return, karate, golf, tennis, volleyball, gymnastics, and **Brain Brawl**—all driven by your real readiness. Train with 3D movement demos and the **Vertical Velocity Academy**’s 12 modules. One number. One ecosystem. Delete the fear.

---

## Back-of-case summary (bullet)

- **Scan** — Get your PRQ, vertical estimate, and movement grade; create and customize your avatar.
- **Train** — Foundations, Flight, and Elite tracks; Movement Snacks; 3D exercise demos and coaching.
- **Play** — 12+ Arena modes: Venice Beach hoops and dunk, Dojo karate, Stadium baseball/football/soccer, golf, tennis, volleyball, gymnastics, and curriculum quiz vs AI.
- **Lab** — Full dunk contest (sprint, gather, fly, stick the landing) and your readiness metrics.
- **Academy** — 12 modules: CNS Freeway, SFMA/FMS, correctives, PJF/TBB, plyos, nutrition.
- **Tap or controller** — Play on phone or with a PS5-style controller; your PRQ shapes every outcome.

**Platform:** iOS (iPhone); path to tvOS/console.  
**Tagline:** *Delete the fear. Your movement, audited.*
