Nexus Engine

FEL's proprietary rendering stack built on SwiftUI Canvas and CADisplayLink. Native 60/120Hz rendering, no game-engine overhead. Every scene is drawn frame-by-frame with full control over physics, particles, and animation.

  • NexusLoop — CADisplayLink at 60/120Hz
  • NexusRenderer — GraphicsContext compositing
  • NexusScene — declarative scene graph
  • Luma AI venue scans
  • Meshy.ai athlete assets
  • Haptic feedback layer
  • AVFoundation IRL camera modes
  • Kai Nexus AI judge system
